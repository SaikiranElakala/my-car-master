# Client information required (nothing below has been invented)

Public contact values are read from environment variables in `frontend/config/client.ts`. The request notification inbox is set to `hello@mycarmaster.in`; confirm the sender domain and delivery in Resend. Confirm the public phone and WhatsApp details with the client before launch.

- Confirmed company phone, email, registered address, WhatsApp number and business hours
- Legal business name, privacy contact / grievance contact, request-record retention period and a process for access, correction and deletion requests
- Confirm which neighborhoods are serviceable within the supplied cities and small areas
- Confirm the supported make/model list if the site should claim specific vehicle coverage; the current list only helps customers enter details, and manual entry remains available
- Confirmed inspection scope and checklist for Used Car Evaluation and New Car PDI
- Whether roadside assistance is 24/7; roadside partners and pricing
- Finance and insurance partners, eligibility rules, approved wording
- Real testimonials (section stays hidden until supplied)
- Real inspection photography and logo files
- Legal review/approval of the Privacy Policy and Terms; define cancellation/refund rules before accepting payments. The Used Car Evaluation page includes a vehicle-scope disclaimer that also needs review. Privacy notice acknowledgement is recorded with requests.
- Razorpay account, Google Analytics, Search Console, WhatsApp Business, email provider accounts (client-owned)
- Final approved price list for each inspection and vehicle segment (current cards and shared pricing configuration conflict; confirm the approved rates)
